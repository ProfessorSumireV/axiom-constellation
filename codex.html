History & Mechanics: The Core of Absolute

ARTIFACT OF THE 301 WIELDERS

// FORGE OF THE VIGINTILLION LIGHTS

Unlike natural cosmic cores born from stellar nucleosynthesis or divine decree, the Core of Absolute is an engineered triumph of mortal ingenuity. It was forged untold eons ago by Antonietti Ernst, the 1st Wielder and prime architect from a multiversal Type-8 Human Civilization. Ernst dismantled technologies from countless multiversal colonies and sacrificed vigintillions of lights, stars, and lives to synthesize an existential engine capable of shattering the hierarchy of gods, Demi-Gods, and Celestials who oppressed humanity.

The Core operates across ten fundamental disciplines of physics: Gravity, Dimension, Creation, Thermodynamic, Acoustic, Quantum, Space, Time, Electromagnetism, and Matter. Each wielder leaves their memory, battle instincts, and final warnings inside the Realm of Absolute. When a wielder focuses purely on one discipline, their eyes shift color: Red for Gravity, Blue for Dimensional, and White for Creation.

// MECHANICAL STAGES, VARIANTS & THE REJECTION PROTOCOL

  • Zeroth Level: Localized black holes, basic spatial displacement, and micro gravitational distortion.
  • 1st to 2nd Level: Neutronium Star compression, Supermassive Black Holes (Sagittarius A*, Phoenix A, TON 618, Dark Phoenix A).
  • 3rd Level: Singularity Rebuild — Infinite-density dimensional collapse that erases Celestials and destroys entire pocket dimensions.
  • 4th to 8th Level: Overhaul of Quantum, Quasar Sovereignty, Mausoleum of Deity sealing, and Definitive Creation (reconstructing shattered universes).
  • 9th to 13th Level: Multiversal Filament Ruptures, Elysian of Decimation, Omnipotence of Destruction, and Truth Ringularity Redundancy.
  • 20th Level (Core of Absolute Truth): Breaking past the limits of mortal logic into an omnipotent blank canvas (achieved solely by Ernst).

Rejection & Variants: When a wielder's body nears expiration, the Core caps energy and detaches to find a new vessel. At Level 5+, SumireV unlocked her Second Variant — Radiant Grim (+40% baseline enhancement across all power, durability, and weapons, projecting a dark misty aura), while Ethan previously unlocked the Hero of Humanity red-crimson variant.

The Oblivion Multiverse & The Cataclysm

EXTINCT EMPIRE OF CALAMITY

// RISE, 9,000-YEAR WAR, & THE SEALING CONTRACT

Originally human stock modified for deep-space survival, the Oblivion expanded across an entire Multiversal Sphere of 10,000 universes, hunting Higher Authorities, Celestials, and Deities. The Land From Up There united neighboring multiverses, Core Absolute divisions, and Demi-Gods against them in a 9,000-year war. At the brink, the Oblivion King detonated their home sphere and sealed it so only true Oblivion blood could enter.

During the final garrison stand of 1,500 warriors, the dying Eclipse people poured their souls into the Sun to bless Ares with Eclipse Oblivion (leaving a mysterious Key mark on his palm and whispering "He is the Key"), binding him by the Oath of Oblivion to bring Calamity upon the Land From Up There.

// THE CATACLYSM ENERGY HIERARCHY & ELDRITCH FORM

  • Cataclysm: Original energy separate from physics, magic, or cosmic radiation; converts any elemental attack into existential erasure. Requires energy tethers attached to the warrior's palms (SumireV lacks this tether restriction).
  • Havoc: Localized annihilation sphere (5s detonation delay after impact) erasing all matter within its bubble.
  • Destructor: Beams, bubbles, tendrils, and orbs that bypass conventional defense; crudely stolen and mimicked by the Arcadian race.
  • Calamity & Eldritch of Oblivion (Ch. 102): Channeled through Ares's dual Annular & Total Eclipse eyes. Inside a Destructor Cocoon, Ares mutates into an Eldritch vessel with a Cataclysm Blade right arm, a glowing Havoc Sphere face, four charred skeleton arms on his back, and realistic multiversal collapse illusions.

// OBLIVION BLOODLINES, AFFILIATES & CASTES

Known Branches & Affiliates: Oblivion Lunar, Oblivion Nebula, Oblivion Stars, Oblivion Solar, Oblivion Occultation, Eclipse People, and Children of Oblivion.

Caste Hierarchy: 1. Baseline Vanguard → 2. The Letheans (Elite deity-hunters) → 3. Primordial Trinitas → 4. The Null Order (Royal bloodline of the King, capable of splitting Heaven Carriers).

The Land From Up There & Cosmic Hierarchy

OPPRESSORS OF MORTALITY (TIERS 01–08)

// THE HIGH CELESTIAL REALM & THE PANTHEON LEAGUE

The Land From Up There sits above mortal multiverses, ruled by the Pantheon League, True Deities, Demi-Gods, and Higher Authorities. They view mortal civilizations as livestock to harvest or prune. In Universe-26, their Celestial Council monitors SumireV, ambushed her in Cluster Creed using Arbiter-11 and later banished her to Universe-4510, while the Arcadians secretly built a Layer 6 Ritual to summon Higher Authorities directly past Ethan's protective cube.

To counter human Principal Ingenuities, Higher Authorities field single-use Archangel artifacts, though they suffer devastatingly from Fallacy of Gods and Inferno Unleash.
